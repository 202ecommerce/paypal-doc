# User documentation

**PayPal module V.6.X for PrestaShop V.1.7.X and above**

As a leader in online payments, PayPal offers a range of simple, seamless and secure solutions trusted by more than 400 million users. With this module, take advantage of our complete payment platform and grow your business online, on mobile and internationally.

This documentation is intended for users of PrestaShop 1.7 and above, using PayPal module version 6 and above.
